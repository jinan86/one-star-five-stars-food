A make.fun bounty entry: one sentence, in the voice of an alien tourist
giving planet Earth a one-star review while still slipping in a real
compliment about the food.

## The entry

> One star: your oceans are absurdly salty and the locals small-talk about
> the weather for hours, yet one bite of rendang made me forgive this
> planet's entire seasoning policy.

174 characters, one sentence. The grievance is specific and vividly alien
(absurdly salty oceans, endless weather small-talk); the turn lands on
rendang — a fresh food angle, not dumplings or noodles — and the complaint
is what makes the compliment hit harder.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13) writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt`
and prints it.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
